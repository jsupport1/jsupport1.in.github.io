Using the Live Currency Converter

Global exchange rates (Forex) change continuously due to interest rates, inflation, trade flows, and macroeconomic conditions. This currency converter helps you quickly convert amounts for over 30 top currencies, including USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, and CAD.

How Offline Caching Works

Unlike standard converters that fail completely when you lose network connectivity, this tool is designed for reliability. Rates are fetched once and saved locally in your browser's localStorage cache. When offline, the tool automatically uses these stored rates, ensuring you can still perform calculations during flights, commutes, or in areas with poor internet connection.
