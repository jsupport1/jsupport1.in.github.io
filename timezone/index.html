Understanding Time Zone Offsets

Converting times across time zones can be confusing due to offsets from Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) and daylight saving adjustments. This time zone converter automatically applies correct offsets to ensure precision.

Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) & GMT

UTC is the international time standard used as a reference point for all time zones. Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) is a timezone that aligns perfectly with UTC, but is subject to Daylight Saving Time adjustments (as British Summer Time, BST) during warm months. Using standard timezone data allows this converter to calculate world times seamlessly without manual math errors.
