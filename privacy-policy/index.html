Privacy Policy

This page explains what information DailyNumberTask collects, how it's used, and your rights.

Information we collect

DailyNumberTask runs primarily in the browser. By default we do not collect numbers you convert. We use analytics and advertising scripts (Google Analytics / Google AdSense) which may collect anonymized usage data and IP-related information. If you interact with third-party features, those services may collect data according to their policies.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies/localStorage to store minimal preferences such as cookie consent and UI choices. Analytics cookies are used by Google Analytics and advertising cookies may be set by Google AdSense. You can decline optional cookies via the banner.

Analytics & advertising

We use Google Analytics for site usage metrics and Google AdSense to serve ads. These services may collect IP addresses and usage signals; see their privacy policies for details.

Advertising

Ad slots on the site are provided by Google AdSense. We don't control individual ad content. We do not place ads directly on interactive controls or in ways that encourage accidental clicks.

Third-party services

We use third-party libraries and services such as Google Analytics, Google AdSense, and YouTube embeds. These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Data retention

We retain analytics data as specified by the third-party providers. User-provided feedback submitted via contact forms is retained until resolved or deleted on request.

Your rights

You can request removal of personal data by contacting the site owner via the Contact page.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data. Note that no online system is perfectly secure — avoid sharing sensitive secrets via the site.
