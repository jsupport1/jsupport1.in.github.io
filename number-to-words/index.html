Number to Words Converter

Convert numbers into properly formatted words for cheques, documents, and reports. Choose Indian or International numbering systems, currency format, and language.

How it works

Numbers are parsed and grouped according to the chosen numbering system. The site uses a local JavaScript engine to map groups to words and handle decimals, negatives, and large integers. Examples below illustrate common cases.

Indian examples

1,25,000 → One Lakh Twenty-Five Thousand

5,00,00,000 → Five Crore

International examples

1,250,000 → One Million Two Hundred Fifty Thousand

1,000,000,000 → One Billion

Cheque & currency

Use the "Cheque / Only" format to add the required suffix. For example: Rupees One Thousand Only.

FAQ

How do I convert decimals to words?

Choose currency or cardinal and enter the decimal number (e.g. 1234.56). The engine will spell sub-units appropriately (Paise, Cents, Pence).
