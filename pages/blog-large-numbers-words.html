Writing Large Numbers in Words: A Banking Guide

Whether you're filling out a deposit slip, writing a cheque, or preparing a legal document, you'll often need to convert large numerical amounts into words. In India, this involves the unique Lakh and Crore system.

The Indian Numbering System

Unlike the Western system that groups numbers in thousands (e.g., 100,000 is one hundred thousand), the Indian system groups the first three digits and then groups subsequent digits in twos.

  • 1,000: One Thousand
  • 1,00,000: One Lakh
  • 1,00,00,000: One Crore

Rules for Writing in Words

When writing these for banking purposes, precision is key:

  • Use 'Only': Always end the sentence with 'Only' to prevent any unauthorized additions.
  • Capitalization: It's a common practice to capitalize the first letter of each major word.
  • Avoid Fractions: If there are paise involved, write them clearly, e.g., "Rupees Five Hundred and Fifty Paise Only."
