Compute maturity value and total interest for your fixed deposit using annual or quarterly compounding.

💡 How It Works

Fixed Deposit (FD) is a savings instrument where you deposit money for a fixed period at a fixed interest rate. The calculator shows your maturity amount, interest earned, and effective yield.

Example: Principal ₹1,00,000 @ 7% for 3 years = Maturity ₹1,22,504 | Interest ₹22,504
