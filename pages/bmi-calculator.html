BMI Calculator

Quickly calculate your Body Mass Index using metric or imperial units, then view your BMI category and healthy range.

💡 How It Works

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight. It helps assess whether your weight is healthy for your height. Categories range from Underweight to Obesity.

Example: Weight 70 kg, Height 175 cm = BMI 22.9 (Normal weight) | Healthy range: 18.5 – 24.9
