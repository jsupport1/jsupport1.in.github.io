NEFT vs RTGS vs IMPS: Which One to Use?

In the digital age, transferring money has become effortless. In India, three primary methods dominate the landscape: NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS. Here's a breakdown of how they differ.

1. NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)

NEFT is a nation-wide payment system that facilitates one-to-one funds transfer. It operates in batches and is ideal for small to medium-sized transfers.

  • Min Transfer: ₹1
  • Max Transfer: No limit (usually)
  • Speed: Settlement in hourly batches
  • Availability: 24/7/365

2. RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)

RTGS is meant for high-value transactions that require immediate clearing. The transfer happens in "real-time" on an individual transaction basis.

  • Min Transfer: ₹2 Lakh
  • Max Transfer: No limit
  • Speed: Instant
  • Availability: 24/7/365

3. IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)

IMPS is managed by NPCI and offers an instant 24/7 interbank electronic fund transfer service through mobile phones.

  • Min Transfer: ₹1
  • Max Transfer: ₹5 Lakh (varies by bank)
  • Speed: Instant
  • Availability: 24/7/365
