The Importance of Your CIBIL Score

If you've ever applied for a credit card or a loan in India, you've likely heard of the CIBIL score. But what exactly is it, and why does it carry so much weight in the banking world?

What is a CIBIL Score?

A CIBIL score is a three-digit numerical summary of your credit history, ranging from 300 to 900. It is calculated based on your past credit behavior, such as loan repayments, credit card usage, and existing debts.

Why Does It Matter?

  • Loan Approval: Banks use your CIBIL score to assess the risk of lending to you. A score above 750 is generally considered excellent and increases your chances of approval.
  • Interest Rates: A higher score can help you negotiate lower interest rates, saving you a significant amount of money over the life of a loan.
  • Credit Card Eligibility: Premium credit cards with better rewards and higher limits often require a superior credit score.

How to Maintain a Good Score

To keep your score high, always pay your EMIs and credit card bills on time. Avoid taking too many loans at once and keep your credit utilization ratio low (ideally below 30%).
