How Business Days are Calculated

Calculating business days or working days is standard across corporate payroll, operations, legal execution windows, and financial institutions. It enables project managers to plan precise timelines without accounting for non-operational days.

Active Work Week Configurations

While a standard corporate week spans Monday to Friday, many global industries run on 6-day models or other shifts (e.g. in retail, production, hospitality, or healthcare). By checking or unchecking specific weekdays, you can tailor this calculator to represent any operational schedule exactly, ensuring 100% accurate results.
